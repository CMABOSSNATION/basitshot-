About Mosai Schools

A Legacy of
Brilliant Minds
in Entebbe

Situated on Garunga Road in the heart of Entebbe, Mosai Schools has been a beacon of quality education for families across the Lake Victoria shoreline and beyond. We believe every child deserves a world-class foundation.

Our dedicated teachers, modern classrooms, and nurturing environment make Mosai the school of choice for progressive Ugandan families who demand excellence without compromise.

Our Programs

Education for Every Stage of Life

From curious toddlers to bright primary graduates — we guide every child's learning journey with love, skill, and dedication.

🍼

Baby & Daycare

A warm, safe and stimulating environment for our youngest learners. Qualified caregivers provide attentive, loving care that gives working parents complete peace of mind.

Nursery School
